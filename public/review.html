#9 SOCIAL ADVANCED🎮 DRAG D2

#10 CORE DESIGN BASIC 1.1
What is a game's "core loop"?
什么是游戏的「核心循环 (core loop)」？
  • ✓ The short repeating sequence of actions a player does most of the time玩家绝大部分时间反复执行的那段短动作序列
  • The opening cutscene that plays once只在开场播一次的过场动画
  • The final boss fight最终 Boss 战
WHY Core loop = the do-it-again unit (aim→shoot→reward→repeat). Everything else wraps around it. 核心循环就是"再来一次"的最小单元，其余系统都包在它外面。
#11 CORE DESIGN BASIC 1.2

在信息流里刷到的小游戏，新玩家理想情况下应在大约多久内看懂？

  • 2–3 minutes after reading instructions读完说明后 2–3 分钟
  • After completing a settings step做完设置步骤之后
WHY Zero-context, near-zero patience: self-explanatory UI beats explanatory text. 零上下文、近乎零耐心，自解释的 UI 胜过解释性文案。
#12 CORE DESIGN ADVANCED 1.3
Which reward schedule tends to be the most engaging (and habit-forming)?
哪种奖励节奏通常最能吸引人（也最易养成习惯）？
  • ✓ Variable / intermittent reward可变（间歇）奖励
  • Fixed reward every single action每个动作都给固定奖励
  • No reward at all完全不给奖励
WHY Variable ratio reinforcement is the strongest driver of repeat behavior. 可变比率强化是重复行为最强的驱动力。

#15 CORE DESIGN ADVANCED 1.6

当难度处于什么状态时，最能维持「心流 (flow)」？

#19 INTERACTION ADVANCED 2.2
Fitts's Law implies that a tap target should be…
Fitts 定律意味着点击目标应当……

  • Always in the exact center永远放在正中心

WHY Time to target ∝ distance / size. Big, near targets are fast. 命中时间正比于距离/尺寸，大而近的目标更快。
